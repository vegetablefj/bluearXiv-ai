Obstructions for codimension one multiple fibers of Lagrangian and Calabi--Yau fibrations ⭐ 精选
Yoon-Joo Kim, Keiji Oguiso, Evgeny Shinder
math.AG
本文证明了紧致hyper-Kähler流形在projective space上的Lagrangian fibration在codimension one中不存在multiple fibers，并由此推进了Sawon关于一般奇异纤维的猜想、Kamenova-Lu的反双曲性以及Néron model作用在base的大开子集上的延拓。对于simply-connected K-trivial variety上的Calabi-Yau fibration（包括elliptic fibrations），除一个例外情形（odd-dimensional K-trivial variety在projective line上具有单个multiplicity 2的fiber，由Borisov-Nuer例子及其推广实现）外，同样成立。该结果与关键词中的hyperkahler和K3 surface相关，为Lagrangian fibration的结构理论提供了重要限制。
A criterion for associated bundles and the weak Beauville--Bogomolov decomposition ⭐ 精选

math.AG

On the decomposition theorem for forgetful maps between moduli spaces of stable pointed curves ⭐ 精选

math.AG
